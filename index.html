Designed and developed a smart, compact, and energy-efficient Continuous Passive Motion (CPM) Machine integrated with IoT technology and a BLDC motor system. The CPM machine was developed to assist patients in rehabilitation therapy by providing controlled and continuous movement for joints during recovery, with a focus on improving efficiency, portability, and smart monitoring capabilities. Key features include IoT-enabled monitoring and control, energy-efficient BLDC motor integration, a compact and portable design, adjustable motion control for patient comfort, and smart automation for rehabilitation support.
